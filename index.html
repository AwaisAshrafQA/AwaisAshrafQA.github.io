Who I Am

Quality from First Test Plan to Release Sign-Off

I'm a Senior SQA Engineer with 7+ years of web, mobile and API quality assurance across fintech, e-commerce, travel, healthcare, logistics and enterprise SaaS.

I raised automation coverage from ~20% to ~85% on frameworks I built from scratch, and introduced AI-driven QA: custom Claude Code skills and LLM agents that turned days of manual effort into minutes.
